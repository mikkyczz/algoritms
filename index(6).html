Algo Lab

A classroom experiment. Scroll a real short-video feed, and find out exactly what a recommendation algorithm learns about you while you do it.

Before you start

This is an educational simulation of what platforms like TikTok, Instagram and YouTube measure in the background every time you scroll. Nothing here is hidden from you — here is exactly what gets recorded:

While you scroll, this app records:
  • How long you watch each video, and which category it belongs to
  • How fast you skip, and how much of each video you actually finish
  • Every like, save and repost you tap
  • Whether you scroll back up to rewatch something
  • Basic device info (screen ratio, touch or mouse)
The feed also adapts in real time — the longer you linger on a category, the more of it you will be served. At the end, your profile summary will be shown on the host's screen for the whole class to discuss.
